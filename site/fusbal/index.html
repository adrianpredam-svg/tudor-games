How to play

Every player stands in a little hole and can't run. When your player has the ball, pull him back with your finger and let go — like a slingshot, the ball flies the other way. Pull further for a harder kick. A slow ball that rolls into any hole is caught by that player, even an opponent! Goalkeepers can't be pulled. You have 15 seconds per kick. First to 3 goals wins.
